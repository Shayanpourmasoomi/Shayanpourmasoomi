## Shayan Pourmasoomi

MSc Economics student at **Sharif University of Technology**, working in **applied microeconomics and urban economics**. I study how transport infrastructure and public policy shape local economic activity, using causal-inference methods, spatial data and large administrative datasets.

**Research interests:** urban and spatial economics · public economics · development economics · economic history · causal inference · machine learning for economics

### Current research

**Do Metro Expansions Increase Local Economic Activity? Satellite and Tax Evidence from Tehran** (with Saeed Tajrishy)  
*Accepted for the Economic Research Forum (ERF) 33rd Annual Conference, April 2027.*

Tehran opened 48 metro stations between 2015 and 2023. I ask whether a new station raises economic activity in the surrounding neighbourhood. The project builds a grid-level spatial panel from VIIRS nighttime-light imagery and administrative tax records, and exploits the staggered timing of openings with the Callaway–Sant'Anna difference-in-differences estimator and event-study designs, so that early- and late-treated areas are compared correctly.

**Other work**

- **From Primary Schools to Secondary Education:** the expansion of Iran's Literacy Corps and educational investment, 1968–1975. Provincial panel built from historical administrative statistics.
- **Long-run educational effects of land reform on rural human capital in Iran.** Triple-differences design using regional variation in policy intensity (proposal).
